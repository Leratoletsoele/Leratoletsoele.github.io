# GoClean — Agile Project Portfolio


Welcome to our GoClean Agile Project Portfolio. This portfolio documents our team's project planning, Agile practices, project evidence and reflections.

GoClean is a proposed laundry pickup and delivery service for university students and busy township residents. The service aims to provide convenience by collecting, washing, drying, folding and delivering customers' laundry.

Our team initially considered a digital public healthcare clinic appointment and patient management system. After considering feedback about feasibility and complexity, we changed our project direction to GoClean.

## Project Overview

- **Project name:** GoClean
- **Project type:** Laundry pickup and delivery service
- **Target customers:** University students and busy township residents
- **Project objective:** To plan and document our project using Agile principles and practices.

## Assessment Sections

### 1. Agile Team Charter

Our project vision, objectives, team members, agreed scope, communication, challenges and reflections on teamwork.

### 2. User Stories and Tasks

User stories describing customer needs, with associated tasks and acceptance criteria.

### 3. Product Backlog

A prioritised list of proposed GoClean features and requirements.

### 4. Sprint Backlog

Tasks selected for sprint work, their responsibilities and progress.

### 5. Agile Logbook

Records of project activities, decisions, progress, challenges and reflections.

## Project Evidence

This portfolio will include relevant project artefacts, such as our presentation, Figma prototype, user stories, backlog records, sprint-planning evidence and logbook entries. Each artefact will be explained and connected to the relevant assessment section.
