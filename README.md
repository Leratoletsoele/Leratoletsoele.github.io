
# GoClean Agile Project Portfolio

Welcome to our GoClean Agile Project Portfolio. This portfolio presents our team's project journey, completed application, Agile practices, supporting evidence and reflections on what we learned throughout the project.

GoClean is a laundry pickup and delivery application designed for university students and busy township residents. It provides a convenient way for customers to explore laundry services and arrange laundry collection and delivery.

Our team initially considered a digital public healthcare clinic appointment and patient management system. After considering lecturer feedback about feasibility and complexity, we changed our project direction to GoClean and focused on a practical laundry service solution.

## Project Overview

- **Project name:** GoClean
- **Project type:** Laundry pickup and delivery application
- **Target customers:** University students and busy township residents
- **Project objective:** To develop GoClean while applying Agile principles and practices to project planning, task organisation, teamwork and reflection.
- **Project management tool:** Trello
- **Application evidence:** GoClean interface screenshots, wireframes and Trello task records

## Project Vision

Our vision is to make laundry services more convenient for students and busy residents by providing a digital experience that supports service selection and laundry pickup and delivery.

## Target Market

GoClean is designed for university students and busy township residents who want a convenient way to manage their laundry alongside their studies, work and daily responsibilities.
