# GoClean — Product Backlog

## 1. Introduction

The product backlog is our prioritised list of proposed features and requirements for GoClean. It helps our team identify the work needed to support the laundry pickup and delivery service and organise that work according to customer needs.

Our Trello board contains a product backlog with 24 cards and separate sprint lists. The items below summarise key features visible on our board and their purpose in the proposed customer journey.

## 2. Product Backlog Items

| Priority | Backlog item | Description | Reason for priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. High | Customer registration | Allow new customers to create an account. | Needed for customers to access account-based features. |
| 2. High | Login page | Allow registered customers to access their accounts securely. | Supports account access and the customer journey. |
| 3. High | Home page | Provide an entry point to the GoClean service. | Helps customers navigate the proposed platform. |
| 4. High | Order page (booking) | Allow customers to provide basket-size and pickup details. | Booking is central to the laundry service. |
| 5. High | Order confirmation screen | Inform customers that a booking has been submitted. | Provides feedback after a booking attempt. |
| 6. High | Order tracking page | Show customers the progress of an order. | Supports communication and order visibility. |
| 7. Medium | Order history page | Let customers review previous orders. | Supports returning customers. |
| 8. Medium | User profile/settings page | Let customers view and manage profile information. | Supports account management. |
| 9. Medium | Notifications system | Communicate relevant order information to customers. | Helps keep customers informed. |
| 10. Medium | Driver assignment | Organise assignment of deliveries to drivers. | Supports the proposed pickup and delivery process. |
| 11. Medium | Payments | Support the proposed payment process. | Relates to paying for the service. |
| 12. Supporting | Marketing strategy | Plan how GoClean will reach its target customers. | Supports awareness and customer acquisition. |
| 13. Supporting | Feasibility and business plan | Document the business concept and its feasibility. | Helps assess the practicality of the proposed service. |
| 14. Supporting | Legal and terms of service | Identify terms and legal considerations for the service. | Helps define the conditions under which the service operates. |

The priority levels above are our proposed ordering based on the intended customer journey. They are not a claim that these exact rankings appear on Trello. Our Trello board remains the record of the backlog cards and sprint organisation maintained by our team.

## 3. How We Organise the Backlog

We use Trello to keep project work visible and organised. The product backlog contains proposed requirements, while the separate sprint lists group features and tasks for planned work.

The board shows items such as the Welcome/Landing Screen, Home Page, Registration Page and Login Page in Sprint 1; Order Page, Order Confirmation, Order Tracking and Order History in Sprint 2; and User Profile/Settings, Notifications, Driver Assignment and Payments in Sprint 3.

This organisation helps our team see how the proposed features are divided into stages. We can review priorities and adjust the backlog when project needs or constraints change.

## 4. Backlog Maintenance

To maintain a useful product backlog, our team should:

- Keep backlog items clear and understandable.
- Prioritise items according to customer value and project needs.
- Break larger features into smaller user stories and tasks.
- Review dependencies between features.
- Update the board when priorities or requirements change.
- Record progress accurately and distinguish proposed work from completed work.

## 5. Reflection

Creating a product backlog has helped me understand how Agile teams organise a project before and during development. Instead of treating the application as one large task, we can identify individual features and consider their importance to customers.

For GoClean, booking is central to the proposed service because customers need a way to request laundry collection. Features such as confirmation and tracking then support the customer after a booking is submitted.

I have also learned that prioritisation should be based on customer value, dependencies and project constraints. A backlog is not simply a list of features; it is something the team reviews and updates as its understanding of the project develops.
